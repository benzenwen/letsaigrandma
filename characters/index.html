Nana

Grandma

One of the first women to earn a Ph.D. in biochemistry, which means she has watched a great many revolutions arrive with press releases. Not against technology. Not impressed by it either. Her question is usually "How would we know?"

Joey

Eleven

Minecraft first, everything else second. Treats AI as a toy and a workshop, tries the ridiculous thing immediately, and occasionally finds something real because nobody else thought to ask. His weakness: a fluent answer feels like a true one.

Jane

Older sister, high school

Suspicious of hype, tired of adults explaining her own generation to her, and worried that AI is making schoolwork and creative work strange. Not against it. Worried that opting out is also a trap.

Mom

Mom

Warm, practical, and the person who actually reads the permission slip.

Dad

Dad

Round yellow glasses. Brings work home, including the parts of work that are now a chatbot.
